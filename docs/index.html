    Reading the chart

    Each line is the chasing team's chance of winning, recalculated after every ball from the scoreboard alone: runs needed, balls left, wickets in hand, the target and the runs and wickets in the most recent few legal balls. Once the result is settled by the rules (target reached, all out, or no balls left) the lines jump to 100% or 0% and the last stretch turns green, marked decided. The top half of the chart is the chasing team's colour and the bottom half the defending team's.

    Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet (ODC-By). ES 335 project by Vinit Parmar and Dutt Parmar. Code, data splits and full results: GitHub repository.
