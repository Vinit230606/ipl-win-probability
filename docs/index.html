Reading the chart

Each line is the chasing team's chance of winning, recalculated after every ball from the scoreboard alone: runs needed, balls left, wickets left, the target and the runs and wickets in the most recent few legal balls. Once the result is settled by the rules (target reached, all out, or no balls left) the lines jump to 100% or 0% and the ball is marked decided. The scoreboard above the chart takes the colour of the side the main model favours.

Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet (ODC-By). ES 335 project by Vinit Parmar and Dutt Parmar. Code, data splits and full results: GitHub repository.
