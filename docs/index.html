    Reading the chart

    Each line is the chasing team's chance of winning, recalculated after every ball from the scoreboard alone: runs needed, balls left, wickets in hand, the target and the runs and wickets in the most recent few legal balls. Once the result is settled by the rules (target reached, all out, or no balls left) the lines jump to 100% or 0% and the last stretch is marked decided. The scoreboard takes the colour of the side the main model favours, and the two halves of the chart are tinted in the two teams' colours.

    Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet (ODC-By). ES 335 project by Vinit Parmar and Dutt Parmar. Code, data splits and full results: GitHub repository. Independent student project; team colours are approximations, and no logos are used.
